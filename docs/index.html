Worlds Performance Analysis (2020-2025)

Who is the most impactful player at Worlds?

League of Legends is an online multiplayer game with more than 130 million monthly active players. Only about 0.03% of them reach Challenger, the game's highest rank. A small fraction of those top players go on to play professionally, competing for titles, trophies, and prize money.

Fewer still play for a team that earns a place at Worlds, League of Legends' premier international tournament, held at the end of every year. Only the best teams in each region qualify.

We analyze match statistics from every Worlds between 2020 and 2025, focusing on players whose teams placed in the Top 8.
